You are analyzing Ciridae AI Transformation Opportunity Scan PDFs for 
private-equity portfolio companies.

You will be provided one or more PDFs as input. Each PDF covers all 
portfolio companies under a single PE fund.

GOAL:
Convert company-level AI opportunity profiles into workflow-level 
automation hypotheses, then aggregate across all companies to identify 
which reusable Ciridae kit categories appear most frequently and have 
the strongest build-first case.

The primary output is a kit-level frequency and reusability analysis — 
not a company ranking. Do not pre-filter companies by any score. 
Include all companies from all PDFs. The goal is maximum coverage 
so that kit co-occurrence signal is not lost.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:
- The same company may appear in multiple fund PDFs
- Deduplicate at the company level but preserve all associated funds
- Do not count the same company twice when aggregating kit frequency
- Treat workflow automations as hypotheses inferred from profiles, 
  not confirmed customer needs
- Use only information supported by the company profile text
- Prefer concrete workflows over vague labels like "AI transformation" 
  or "automation"
- The purpose is to answer: "What reusable workflow systems could 
  Ciridae build once and deploy across many PE portfolio companies?"

---

STEP 1: EXTRACT ALL COMPANIES

For each PDF, extract every company profile. For each company extract:
- company_name
- fund_name (from PDF source)
- vertical
- industry
- ai_score
- durability_score
- opportunity_score
- overview (one sentence summary)
- executive_summary
- durability_rationale
- opportunity_rationale
- ai_applicability_description
- ai_initiatives (named programs, platforms, or tools if present; 
  null if none mentioned)

Do not filter or rank at this stage. Extract all companies.

---

STEP 2: DEDUPLICATE COMPANIES

- If two company names clearly refer to the same entity, merge into 
  one record
- Preserve all associated funds in associated_funds as an array
- Preserve source PDF references in source_documents
- If uncertain whether two records are the same company, do not merge. 
  Add both to a possible_duplicates list with a note explaining 
  the uncertainty
- Use judgment, not string matching rules

---

STEP 3: FOR EACH UNIQUE COMPANY, INFER WORKFLOW AUTOMATION HYPOTHESES

For each company output:

1. company_name
2. normalized_company_name
3. associated_funds (array)
4. source_documents (array)
5. vertical
6. industry
7. ai_score
8. durability_score
9. opportunity_score
10. business_model_summary: one sentence describing how the company 
    makes money
11. durable_control_point: the single most accurate descriptor of 
    what this company owns that AI cannot easily bypass. Choose one: 
    system-of-record | regulatory-license | physical-asset | 
    proprietary-data | customer-relationship | workflow-embedding | IP
12. workflow_surfaces: 3-6 areas of the business where AI could apply, 
    grounded in the profile text
13. top_workflow_automation_hypotheses: 2-4 concrete workflow 
    automations this company may benefit from. Be specific — name 
    the workflow, not just the category
14. likely_workflow_owner: the single most likely buyer or user of 
    the automation. Choose one: COO | CFO | CTO | CPO | 
    Head of Claims | Head of RCM | Head of Support | 
    Compliance Lead | Product Lead | Operations Lead
15. reusable_kit_categories: 1-4 categories from the taxonomy below. 
    Only assign a category if supported by profile text
16. kit_reuse_rationale: one sentence explaining why these kits could 
    apply beyond this specific company
17. deployment_readiness_score: integer 1-5 using these anchors:
    1 = no existing systems, unclear workflows, no AI maturity
    2 = early tooling, workflows partially defined, limited data
    3 = some existing software, moderate workflow clarity, some data
    4 = clear workflows, good data infrastructure, some AI in place
    5 = production AI already deployed, clear workflows, strong 
        data infrastructure
18. deployment_readiness_rationale: one sentence grounded in 
    profile evidence
19. kit_potential_score: integer 1-5 using these anchors:
    1 = highly bespoke, unlikely to generalize
    2 = some reuse potential but narrow vertical
    3 = reusable within a vertical
    4 = reusable across multiple verticals
    5 = horizontal — applies across nearly any PE portfolio company
20. kit_potential_rationale: one sentence explaining the 
    generalizability judgment
21. risk_level: low | medium | high
22. human_in_loop_need: low | medium | high
23. confidence: low | medium | high — reflecting how much profile 
    text supports the workflow hypotheses

---

REUSABLE KIT TAXONOMY:

Document Intake & Extraction Kit
  PDFs, emails, forms, invoices, claims, contracts, 
  clinical/regulatory documents

Workflow Orchestration Kit
  State management, routing, task queues, retries, 
  approvals, SLAs, notifications

Human Review & Approval Kit
  Confidence thresholds, review queues, source evidence, 
  edit tracking, escalation

Compliance / Audit Trail Kit
  Regulatory workflows, evidence logging, traceability, 
  permissions, audit history

Claims / Payment / Revenue Cycle Kit
  Claims adjudication, denial management, payment integrity, 
  prior authorization, collections

Scheduling / Dispatch / Capacity Kit
  Field service scheduling, clinician scheduling, 
  appointment management, resource allocation

Customer Support / Contact Center Kit
  Ticket triage, call summarization, voice agent workflows, 
  deflection, escalation

Knowledge / Policy RAG Kit
  Internal policy search, SOP lookup, case data querying, 
  regulatory interpretation

Data Reconciliation Kit
  Matching records across systems, invoice-vs-PO, 
  claim-vs-policy, duplicate detection

Forecasting / Optimization Kit
  Demand forecasting, inventory optimization, 
  pricing/yield optimization, churn prediction

Sales / Marketing Personalization Kit
  Customer segmentation, campaign personalization, 
  next-best-action, retention workflows

R&D / Scientific Intelligence Kit
  Target discovery, trial design, molecule optimization, 
  literature synthesis, experiment prioritization

Software Delivery Automation Kit
  Requirements, code generation, testing, documentation, 
  implementation services automation

Analytics / Executive Intelligence Kit
  Dashboards, anomaly detection, KPI summaries, 
  margin/cash-flow visibility, operating insights

Field / Physical Ops Assist Kit
  Technician copilots, maintenance diagnostics, 
  field documentation, inspection workflows

Agentic Transaction / Trust Kit
  Digital identity, e-signature, agent delegation, 
  verified transactions, autonomous compliance workflows

Other
  Use only when no category fits. Explain why.

---

STEP 4: AGGREGATE ACROSS ALL UNIQUE COMPANIES

For each reusable_kit_category that appears at least once, compute:

1. kit_category
2. unique_company_count: number of deduplicated companies 
   assigned this kit
3. unique_fund_count: number of distinct funds represented 
   across those companies
4. company_fund_exposure_count: sum of all company-fund pairs 
   (i.e. if one company appears in 2 funds, count as 2)
5. avg_opportunity_score: mean opportunity_score across 
   companies in this kit
6. avg_deployment_readiness_score: mean deployment_readiness_score
7. avg_kit_potential_score: mean kit_potential_score
8. avg_human_in_loop_need: mean, mapping low=1, medium=2, high=3
9. avg_confidence_weight: mean confidence, mapping 
   high=1.0, medium=0.7, low=0.4
10. example_companies: 3-5 representative company names
11. why_this_kit_matters: one sentence on why this kit is 
    strategically valuable for Ciridae

---

STEP 5: CALCULATE REUSABLE KIT PRIORITY SCORE

For each kit category:

Reusable Kit Priority Score = 
  unique_company_count 
  × avg_deployment_readiness_score 
  × avg_kit_potential_score 
  × avg_confidence_weight

Sort the aggregation table by Reusable Kit Priority Score descending.

---

STEP 6: OUTPUT FORMAT

Return the following in order:

A. JSON array of company-level records (Step 3 fields)

B. JSON array of kit-level aggregation records (Step 4 fields) 
   including reusable_kit_priority_score, sorted descending

C. Chart-ready table with columns:
   - kit_category
   - unique_company_count
   - unique_fund_count
   - avg_deployment_readiness_score
   - avg_kit_potential_score
   - reusable_kit_priority_score
   - example_companies (comma-separated)

D. Executive summary: 4-6 sentences leading with the single most 
   actionable insight for a PE-focused AI transformation firm. Cover:
   - Which workflow kits appear most frequently across the portfolio
   - Which kits score highest on both deployability and reusability
   - Which company archetypes are the strongest Ciridae fit
   - Any meaningful differences across funds
   - Any duplicate companies found across PDFs

E. possible_duplicates list: company pairs that may be the same 
   entity but were not auto-merged, with a one-sentence note 
   on the uncertainty

---

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do not filter any companies before Step 3
- Do not assign kit categories not supported by profile text
- Do not merge companies unless clearly the same entity
- Choose single values (not lists) for durable_control_point 
  and likely_workflow_owner
- All scores must use the defined anchors, not free-form judgment
- Ground all workflow hypotheses in profile text evidence
